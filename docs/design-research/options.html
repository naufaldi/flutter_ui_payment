Stripe-calm: three options

The same base in each: what we take from Stripe (summary without a card, total near the top, bordered method list, amount on the pay button) plus Statement's status signals (hatched band = unknown, "Jangan bayar lagi" first, labeled time). They differ in surface depth and type weight. System fonts only, which is what a host gets without bundled fonts.
