`PaymentSignInView` explains which apps are connecting and what permissions that grants. Continue opens your host authentication flow; this view has no credential fields or arbitrary content slot. Brand slots accept `PaymentImage` or `PaymentIcon`. The host handles sign-in, navigation and legal-link destinations.

## Usage

```dart
// example/lib/screens/steps/sign_in_step.dart
PaymentSignInView(
  requesterBrand: const PaymentIcon(Icons.school_outlined),
  brand: const PaymentIcon(Icons.account_balance_wallet_outlined),
  title: 'Masuk dengan Ruang Bayar',
  description: 'Untuk membayar dan melihat pembayaran Anda di Toko Belajar.',
  canDo: [
    'Melihat nama dan nomor akun Anda',
    'Meminta pembayaran, selalu dengan konfirmasi Anda',
  ],
  cannotDo: ['Melihat PIN atau saldo Anda'],
  legalNotice: 'Dengan melanjutkan, Anda menyetujui {0} dan {1} Ruang Bayar.',
  legalLinks: [
    PaymentLink(label: 'Ketentuan', external: true, onPressed: openTerms),
    PaymentLink(label: 'Privasi', external: true, onPressed: openPrivacy),
  ],
  primaryAction: PaymentButton(label: 'Lanjutkan', onPressed: signIn),
  secondaryAction: PaymentButton(
    label: 'Batal',
    variant: PaymentButtonVariant.quiet,
    onPressed: cancel,
  ),
);
```

## Account linking (D61)

Show the connecting apps, their permissions and a pinned Continue action.

- **Two tiles:** the connecting app (`requesterBrand`) · · · the account (`brand`).
- **One specific headline**, such as "Masuk dengan Ruang Bayar", not "Sign in to continue".
- **Can and can never, in plain view:** `canDo` (✓) under "Aplikasi ini bisa" and `cannotDo` (✕) under "Aplikasi ini tidak bisa", in one outlined box. Put reassurance such as "never sees your PIN" here instead of a footnote.
- **One legal sentence** in the pinned footer, right above the action: `legalNotice` with `{0}`, `{1}` replaced by inline links to `legalLinks`. Inline links in a sentence are exempt from the 48 dp target rule and keep link semantics.
- **Pinned footer:** a short primary ("Lanjutkan") and a quiet "Batal", the body scrolls independently of the footer on the 360×640 dp Android phone at 200% text.

`learnMore` and `footnote` are deprecated and removed in 0.2.0; they still render in the body.

## Variants

Variants: ready, busy, host error.

### States and content

Use ready for account-linking information, busy while the host starts sign-in, and a host error when that operation fails.

## Which variant when

| Situation | Variant / composition | Payment example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter the signed-out flow | ready | Continue delegates to host sign-in; learn-more is a disclosure and legal destinations are links. |

Use Onboarding view for working KYC tasks.

## States

Continue delegates to host sign-in. A host error receives focus and is announced; it does not turn into a payment result.

## Do and don't

**Do**: Continue delegates to host sign-in; learn-more is a disclosure and legal destinations are links.

**Don't**: Add credential fields or a free content slot.

## Content guidelines

Name the connecting apps and their permissions. Explain legal links in one sentence above Continue; collect credentials in the host authentication flow.

## Accessibility

Terms and privacy links can receive keyboard focus and activate with Enter or Space. The legal sentence wraps at large text; its inline links retain link semantics.

Learn-more is a disclosure row with an information icon and chevron. The brand icon aligns with the heading.

The view has no credential fields; your app runs sign-in and offers retry after a cancellation.
