`PrivacyMask` hides what it wraps behind a plain surface and a lock icon while `obscured` is true. Use it for account numbers, identity details and balances that should not show in the app switcher or on a shared screen.

<HostOwns>when to obscure (usually from the app lifecycle) and platform screenshot protection. The mask covers the Flutter content; it does not stop the operating system from capturing the screen.</HostOwns>

## Usage

```dart
PrivacyMask(
  obscured: lifecycle != AppLifecycleState.resumed,
  child: accountDetails,
);
```

`lifecycle` is your own state, kept from an `AppLifecycleListener` or `WidgetsBindingObserver`; `accountDetails` is the content to hide.

## Obscured

The child stays laid out underneath, so nothing jumps when the mask lifts. The cover uses `PaymentTheme.surface` with a 48 px lock icon in `textSecondary`.

## Content guidelines

- Mask whole blocks, not single digits. A half-covered number still leaks.
- Unmask as soon as the app is active again. The mask is not a lock screen; it asks for nothing.

## Accessibility

- While obscured, the child is excluded from semantics and screen readers hear "Content hidden" instead.
