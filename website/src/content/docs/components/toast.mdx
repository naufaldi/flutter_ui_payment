## Variants

Variants: neutral, info, success, warning, danger; pending. Nothing else exists.

### Tones

`tone` selects the icon. Ink tokens provide textPrimary fill and surface text in both themes.

| Tone | Icon |
| --- | --- |
| `neutral` (default), `info` | `Icons.info_outline` |
| `success` | `Icons.check_circle_outline` |
| `warning` | `Icons.warning_amber_outlined` |
| `danger` | `Icons.error_outline` |

### With undo

Pass both `actionLabel` and `onAction` to add an action. If either is missing, no action is shown.

```dart
showPaymentToast(
  context,
  message: 'Promo hidden',
  actionLabel: 'Undo',
  onAction: () => host.restorePromo(promo.id),
);
```

### Pending and reading time

Stripe none, success and caution map to neutral, success and warning. Pending uses a small spinner and never times out. A blocking failure belongs in a banner.

```dart
final toast = showPaymentToast(context, message: 'Refreshing balance…', pending: true);
toast.update('Balance updated', tone: StatusTone.success);
```

Short messages last 4 seconds, or 6 with an action. Longer messages get 1 second plus 300 ms per word, capped at 10 seconds. An action remains available during accessible navigation. Ink toasts use textPrimary fill and surface text, with no shadow. Aim for about 30 characters. Updates keep the same toast on screen; dismiss is safe to repeat.

### Replacing a toast

Only the newest toast counts. Showing a toast replaces the kit toast on screen and drops any kit toast still waiting, so feedback never waits behind another toast's reading time (D77). The toast on screen leaves over 150 ms, then the new one enters. With reduced motion the swap is instant. A replaced toast's controller stops responding: `update` and `dismiss` do nothing, and `closed` completes when it leaves. A waiting kit toast dropped by your app's `clearSnackBars()` completes when a later kit toast reaches the front; a dropped waiting legacy `showPaymentSnackBar` future never completes. A controller keeps working until its toast closes, even after a route with its own `Scaffold` is popped.

If your app also calls `ScaffoldMessenger.showSnackBar`, a kit toast replaces your snack bar when it is on screen. When reduced motion turns on or off, and before the first kit toast on a messenger, the kit clears the whole snack bar queue, your bars included (D78). Flutter rebuilds its snack bar animation when the timing changes, and a bar left waiting would freeze the queue. A dropped bar's `closed` never completes. If your own bars pass `snackBarAnimationStyle`, the kit can't see that change.

## States

Host state is explicit. Loading does not imply success; disabled actions retain a readable explanation. Interactive controls retain focus and pressed feedback.

**Layout.** The icon or spinner and the action centre on the message's first line, so a short toast reads on one line and a long one keeps the icon beside its first line.

## Do and don't

**Do**: Use short outcome copy; pending controllers can update or dismiss host work.

**Don't**: Replace persistent payment status with a transient toast.

## Content guidelines

- Say what happened in the past tense, in a few words: "Reference copied", "Card removed".
- One message per toast. Do not put instructions or errors that need attention in a toast; it disappears. Use a [notice banner](/components/banner/) instead.
- Never report a payment result in a toast. Payment status belongs on the [status screen](/patterns/status-receipt/).
- Action labels are one verb: "Undo", "View".

Use short outcome copy; pending controllers can update or dismiss host work.

## Accessibility

- Screen readers announce snack bars automatically.
- The tone adds an icon, so the message is not carried by color alone. The icon is excluded from semantics; the message is what is read.

## Migration

`showPaymentSnackBar` remains a deprecated wrapper returning the Material controller until 0.2.0. Use `showPaymentToast` for pending, update and dismiss.

A replaced toast's legacy controller closes with `SnackBarClosedReason.hide`. One dropped from behind your own snack bar when reduced motion changes never completes.
