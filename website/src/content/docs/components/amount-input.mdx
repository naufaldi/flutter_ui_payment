`PaymentCurrencyField` lets people type an amount, for a top-up or a transfer. It parses strictly for the current locale and reports the result on every change; it never guesses what an unclear amount means.

<HostOwns>the currency, the minimum and maximum, and whether a valid amount may be paid. Enable your confirm action only for a valid result.</HostOwns>

## Usage

```dart
PaymentCurrencyField(
  currency: 'IDR',
  minimum: const Money.idr(10000),
  maximum: const Money.idr(5000000),
  onChanged: (result) => setState(() => amount = result),
);

PaymentButton(
  label: 'Continue',
  onPressed: amount.isValid ? () => host.quote(amount.money!) : null,
);
```

Keep the latest `AmountParseResult` in your state. Exactly one of `money` and `issue` is set, and `isValid` is true when `money` is.

## Default

The field shows the label "Amount" (or your `label`), the currency symbol as a prefix, and the entered amount in the 20 px amount style with tabular figures. With a `minimum` or `maximum`, a helper line shows the limits, for example "Min. Rp10,000 · Max. Rp5,000,000" in English. `initialText` fills the field when it first appears.

## Errors

Every change is parsed, but the error text appears only after the field loses focus with text in it, after the keyboard's done action, or when you set `showErrors: true`, for example after a submit attempt. People are not told they are wrong while they are still typing.

| `AmountIssue` | When | Message (English) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `empty` | Nothing entered | Enter an amount. |
| `invalidFormat` | Letters or misplaced separators | Use digits only, for example 150000. |
| `ambiguous` | Text that could be read two ways, such as `100,000` for IDR in Indonesian | This format can be read two ways. Remove separators and try again. |
| `negative` | A minus sign | Amount can’t be negative. |
| `zero` | Zero | Amount must be more than 0. |
| `belowMinimum` | Under `minimum` | Minimum amount is Rp10,000. |
| `aboveMaximum` | Over `maximum` | Maximum amount is Rp5,000,000. |

## Parsing

The currency symbol or code, spaces and correctly placed group separators are accepted, so pasted amounts work. Separators follow the locale (see [Money](/foundations/money/)). Currencies without minor units, such as IDR, reject a decimal part as ambiguous rather than reading it as a group separator. The keyboard offers a decimal key only when the currency has minor units.

## Content guidelines

- Keep the default label unless the amount has a specific role, such as "Top-up amount".
- Always pass the limits you enforce, so people see them before they type.
- Do not format or rewrite what people type; show the formatted amount elsewhere, for example in the [summary](/components/checkout/).

## Accessibility

- The entered amount follows text scaling up to 1.6×, like other large amounts.
- Error text is shown under the field in the danger tone and wraps up to three lines.

## Behavior, states and migration

Stripe CurrencyField maps to PaymentCurrencyField; description appears above limits. Size keeps the amountLarge style: small subtracts 8 dp from today's default input padding, medium keeps it, large adds 8 dp; even small stays at least 48 dp. Helpers and errors can add height.

PadAllFractionDigits maps to padFractionDigits: on blur or submit, valid USD 5 becomes 5.00 (5,00 in Indonesian); IDR and invalid entries stay unchanged. This is formatting only and emits no onChanged callback. FractionDigitOffset is refused (D28). Currency allowlists arrive in Phase 4B; parse issues and values remain AmountParseResult.

Migration: AmountInput remains a deprecated typedef until 0.2.0. Rename the constructor to PaymentCurrencyField; existing arguments keep compiling.

The tested fixture with description and minimum helper measures small 92, medium 100 and large 108 dp at 1×; helper content contributes to these totals.

Host errorText overrides parse issues; hiddenElements keeps hidden subtext spoken. Errors use an icon and danger edge through the shared field treatment.
