`StatusHeader` is the top of a status screen: a thin band, then an icon beside the state in words. Band shape, tone, icon and words are four signals, so a hatched band (the outcome is not known yet) never reads as a plain "pending".

## Usage

```dart
const StatusHeader(
  tone: StatusTone.warning,
  icon: Icons.help_outline,
  title: 'Result not confirmed yet',
  hatched: true,
);
```

`PaymentStatusView`, `PaymentReceipt` and `TransactionDetail` already use it. Use it directly for your own status screens so they match.

## Solid

The default. A solid band marks a settled state (success, failed) or one that is waiting on something known, such as a transfer the person still has to make.

## Hatched

`hatched: true` draws diagonal stripes, like caution tape. Use it only when the payment was sent and no result has arrived: the outcome is unknown, and the person must not pay again. The difference from "waiting" is in shape and words as well as in color.

## Band only

`StatusBand` is the 6 px band on its own, for a layout that puts the title elsewhere. It takes the same `tone` and `hatched`.

```dart
const StatusBand(tone: StatusTone.success);
```

## Content guidelines

- The title is the state in plain words: "Payment received", "Payment failed", "Result not confirmed yet".
- Do not use "Processing" for an unknown outcome. Say that the result is not confirmed, and say what to do (see [Payment attempt and status](/concepts/payment-attempt/)).
- Pick an icon that agrees with the title.

## Accessibility

- The title is marked as a header for screen readers. The band is decorative and excluded from semantics.
- The title scales with the text and wraps. The icon grows up to 1.6× and stays centered on the title's first line.
