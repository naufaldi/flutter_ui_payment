`ActionTile` is a tappable row for settings, menus and action lists: an outlined icon, a title and a muted detail line, with no box around it.

## Usage

```dart
ActionTileGroup(children: [
  ActionTile(
    icon: Icons.receipt_long_outlined,
    title: 'Transaction history',
    subtitle: 'Last 90 days',
    onTap: openHistory,
  ),
  const ActionTile(
    icon: Icons.pin_outlined,
    title: 'Change PIN',
    disabledReason: 'Managed by your bank app',
  ),
]);
```

Put consecutive tiles in an `ActionTileGroup`. It draws a 1 px `outline` hairline between rows and no box around them, so the group sits on the page like a [section](/components/section/) and nests inside your own cards without stacking borders.

## Default

With `onTap`, the row shows a chevron at the end (mirrored in right-to-left languages). Pass `trailing` to put your own widget there instead, such as a value or a 48 dp button; it keeps its own size.

## Disabled

A row without `onTap` is disabled. It stays visible with a muted title, but it is not focusable. Pass `disabledReason`; it replaces the subtitle so people know why they cannot use the row.

## Destructive

`destructive: true` draws the icon and title in the danger tone. Color is never the only signal: the title must already say what happens, such as "Sign out of all devices". Confirm the action with a [dialog](/components/dialog/) before you run it.

```dart
ActionTile(
  icon: Icons.logout,
  title: 'Sign out of all devices',
  destructive: true,
  onTap: confirmSignOut,
);
```

## Content guidelines

- Titles name the destination or the action: "Transaction history", "Change PIN". Use sentence case and no end punctuation.
- Keep the subtitle to one short line of extra context, such as "Last 90 days".
- Write disabled reasons as the cause or the way out: "Managed by your bank app", "Verify your identity first".
- Use an icon on every row of a group or on none, so titles line up.

## Accessibility

- Rows are at least 48 dp tall and announce as buttons; disabled rows announce as disabled.
- The icon and chevron center on the title's first line, so they stay beside the title when it wraps at large text. The icon grows with text up to 1.6×.
- Keyboard focus draws the kit's [focus ring](/foundations/focus/).
