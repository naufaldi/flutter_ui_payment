Labels identify a value after typing begins. Hints are examples, not labels. Show business validation on submit, then update it after edits. Focus the first invalid field in reading order. Use [Form field group](/components/form-field-group/) legends for related facts, 24 dp apart. Dates are typed first; the date of birth's calendar button opens a wheel and a near date opens a calendar ([Date field](/components/date-field/)). On a phone the province search opens a full-height sheet ([Search field](/components/search-field/)).

The KYC form in H04/K01 groups name, masked-in-review NIK and birth date under Personal details. Work and address contains occupation (short Select) and province (long searchable list). The host supplies all options and validation; the kit formats dates and renders errors.

```dart
// example/lib/screens/steps/kyc_fields.dart
IdentityFieldSpec(
  key: 'province', label: s.province, group: s.groupWorkAddress,
  kind: IdentityFieldKind.search, options: _provinces,
  validator: (v) => v.isEmpty ? s.provinceRequired : null,
);
```

D1a: do not collect PIN, OTP, passwords or card secrets with these fields. H04 sign-in delegates to the host with no credential inputs. Reports in A02 keep free text local and send only category and length; never log what a customer typed.
