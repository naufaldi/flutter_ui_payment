Show a confirmed payment and its refund. The original payment keeps its success
and amount in every refund state. The refund is a separate section with its own
status, amount and masked reference, all from the host. The person can ask for a
newer refund status and open a read-only receipt.

## Run and try

From `example/`:

```sh
flutter run -d <device-id> -t lib/refund_detail_demo.dart
```

Tap **Perbarui status refund**: the synthetic host moves the refund from pending
to partial. Tap **Lihat struk** for the receipt. The developer menu offers
**Refund penuh** (the refund completes for the whole amount) and **Cek refund
gagal** (the first refresh fails, the last known refund status stays and the next
refresh succeeds). Changing a scenario starts a fresh synthetic session.

## Composition and boundaries

`PaymentRefundDetail` takes the host's `TransactionRecord` with its `RefundInfo`
and callbacks for refreshing and copying. `PaymentRefundReceiptSheet` shows the
host's `PaymentReceiptData`. The refresh action disappears once the refund is
final.

| UI | Existing library surface |
| --- | --- |
| Original payment, refund section and copy | `TransactionDetail`, `TransactionRecord`, `RefundInfo` |
| Receipt sheet | `PaymentReceipt`, `PaymentReceiptData`, `PaymentSheet` (opened by the host with `showPaymentSheet`) |
| Pinned actions and refresh failure | `PaymentFullPageView`, `PaymentBanner` |

This block never starts, edits or cancels a refund. The host owns refund
requests, their status, masking and the clipboard. All records are fictional.
See the [transaction history pattern](/patterns/history/) and the
[status and receipt pattern](/patterns/status-receipt/).

## Proof and limits

Widget tests cover the original payment remaining unchanged, failed refund-check recovery and a reachable refresh action at 320 dp with 200% text. Connect your own refund-status service; this block does not initiate refunds. See [Compatibility](/reference/compatibility/) for device coverage.
