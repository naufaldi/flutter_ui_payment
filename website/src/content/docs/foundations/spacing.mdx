---
title: Spacing and radius
description: One spacing unit used in multiples by role, three corner radii derived from one token, and 48 dp targets.
---

Two `PaymentTheme` tokens set the kit's layout: `spacing`, the base unit (default 8), and `radius`, the corner radius of selectable boxes (default 8). Everything else is a multiple of them, so changing one token keeps every screen in proportion.

```dart
final theme = PaymentTheme.of(context);

Padding(
  padding: EdgeInsets.all(theme.spacing * 2), // group: 16
  child: DecoratedBox(
    decoration: BoxDecoration(
      border: Border.all(color: theme.border),
      borderRadius: theme.controlBorderRadius, // 6
    ),
    child: child,
  ),
);
```

## Spacing

Layouts use multiples of `spacing` by role, not by eye:

| Role | Value | Multiple | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| inline | 4 | `spacing * 0.5` | Label to caption, title to meta line, icon to badge text. |
| related | 8 | `spacing` | Row padding, between stacked actions. |
| block | 12 | `spacing * 1.5` | Band to title, notice padding, icon to row text. |
| group | 16 | `spacing * 2` | Screen and sheet gutter, between groups. |
| section | 24 | `spacing * 3` | Content to the primary action, ruled gaps between sections. |
| major | 32 | `spacing * 4` | Empty and state message padding only. |

Sections are separated by space, not boxes. When a rule is needed between sections, a [divider](/components/divider/) sits in the middle of a 24 dp gap.

## Radius

| Getter | Value at `radius: 8` | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tagBorderRadius` | 4 | Badges, tags and skeleton blocks. |
| `controlBorderRadius` | 6 | Buttons, inputs, notices and menus. |
| `borderRadius` | 8 | Selectable boxes such as the method list. |
| `sheetBorderRadius` | 12 | The top of a sheet, and dialogs. |

When you nest a rounded element inside another, give the inner one the smaller radius so the corners stay concentric.

## Targets

Everything people tap is at least 48 by 48 dp: buttons, rows, tabs, checkbox, radio and switch rows, and icon buttons. Rows grow taller at large text; they never shrink below 48.

## Content guidelines

- Use the roles above in your own screens instead of new values. A layout of 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 reads as one system.
- Keep the 16 dp gutter at every width. On a 320 dp phone it still leaves 288 dp for content.
- Put the most space before the primary action (24), so it reads as the end of the screen.
