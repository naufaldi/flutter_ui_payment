Every kit widget takes its colors from `PaymentTheme`. A widget never picks a color by itself, so a brand is a change of tokens, not a fork. To install a brand, see [Theming](/concepts/theming/).

```dart
final theme = PaymentTheme.of(context);
final warning = theme.tone(StatusTone.warning);

Text('Total changed', style: TextStyle(color: warning.foreground));
```

`PaymentTheme.of` returns the extension on your `ThemeData`, or `PaymentTheme.fromColorScheme` of your `ColorScheme` when none is installed.

## Where the values come from

- **Status tones** (`info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`) are fixed, contrast-checked values in `PaymentTheme.fromColorScheme`, whatever your seed color is. Their containers mix the foreground into your `surface`: 90% of the way to the surface in light mode, 84% in dark mode.
- **Every other token** comes from your `ColorScheme`, or from what you set with `copyWith`. The tables below show the example app's Hairline brand (`example/lib/themes.dart`) so you can see a complete, contrast-checked set. Your values will differ.

Use `outline` when the line only separates content and nothing breaks if someone cannot see it. Use `border` when the line is how someone finds a control. When you do not set `border`, it defaults to `textSecondary` a quarter of the way to `surface`.

## Status tones

Each tone is a `ToneColors` pair: a `foreground` for icons and text, and a `container` for the fill behind them. Foregrounds are the same for every host. Containers below are computed for the Hairline surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#131417`).

`neutral` follows your scheme: `onSurfaceVariant` for the foreground and `surfaceContainerHigh` for the container. It has no fixed value to show.

A tone never carries a status alone. `StatusBadge`, `NoticeBanner`, `StatusHeader` and `showPaymentSnackBar` always pair the tone with an icon and words, and the [status band](/components/status-header/) adds a shape: hatched when the outcome is not known yet. Do the same in your own screens.

## Contrast rules

- Text reaches 4.5:1 on the background it sits on. Each fixed tone foreground reaches at least 5.8:1 on white and 7.6:1 on `#131417`.
- Control edges (`border`) and meaningful icons reach 3:1.
- Decorative rules (`outline`) may be lower, because nothing depends on seeing them.
- Disabled controls are exempt, but `disabled` stays readable on `surfaceMuted`.
