## One payment, a lost response, no second payment offered

This is the example app's checkout demo. The submit reaches the server, the response never comes back, and the screen recovers without offering a second payment.

<HomeFlow />

The demo runs on an Android emulator or an iOS Simulator with synthetic data and a fake host. No money moves and no network calls are made. [Walk through the demo](/blocks/checkout-recovery/) or [run it yourself](/start/try-it/).

## The hard states are already designed

Happy-path checkout screens are easy. These are the cases that cost money and support time. The kit has a designed answer for each:

<Points>
  <Point title="The submit timed out after the server accepted it">
    The screen leads with “Don’t pay again.”, shows a hatched band that never looks like ordinary pending, and offers one action: check status, using the same attempt reference.
  </Point>
  <Point title="An older status arrives after a newer one">
    `PaymentAttempt.reconcile` rejects stale revisions, another attempt’s data, a mismatched server reference, and a success that turns into anything else without an official provider correction.
  </Point>
  <Point title="The total changed after the customer chose a method">
    `PaymentConfirmation` shows the old and new total and keeps confirm unreachable until the customer accepts the new terms.
  </Point>
  <Point title="The payment is still pending or unknown">
    No pay-again action. A new attempt is offered only when the host allows it and the previous attempt is final and not a success. Your backend still enforces idempotency.
  </Point>
</Points>

```dart
// Every status response goes through reconcile. Rejected snapshots leave the
// attempt as it was and tell you why.
final result = attempt.reconcile(snapshotFromYourAdapter);
if (!result.applied) debugPrint('ignored: ${result.rejection}');
attempt = result.attempt;

PaymentStatusView(
  attempt: attempt,
  amount: quote.total,
  onAction: (action) => host.handle(action), // only attempt.availableActions
);
```

## Built to fit your app, not replace it

<Points>
  <Point title="Compose what you need">
    Use single [components](/components/), follow the [patterns](/patterns/checkout/), or copy a whole [block](/blocks/) such as checkout and recovery, then wire your own callbacks.
  </Point>
  <Point title="Your brand, light and dark">
    `PaymentTheme` is a `ThemeExtension`. Without it the kit follows your `ColorScheme`; with it you set colors, radius and amount style for light and dark. [Theming](/concepts/theming/)
  </Point>
  <Point title="Your backend stays in charge">
    Widgets render the data you pass and report what the customer did through callbacks. No network calls, no payment SDK, no router or state manager required.
  </Point>
  <Point title="English and Indonesian included">
    Strings, money separators and dates are localized for `en` and `id`. Register `PaymentLocalizations.delegate` and pick the locale. [Localization](/foundations/localization/)
  </Point>
  <Point title="Accessibility is documented, not implied">
    Status never relies on color alone, focus is visible, and the captured checkout and status screens reflow at 200% text with the key action still reachable. [Accessibility](/concepts/accessibility/)
  </Point>
  <Point title="Optional KYC, same rules">
    Identity capture, review and onboarding screens show progress and structured errors. The decision and document validation stay with your backend. [KYC capture](/patterns/kyc-capture/)
  </Point>
</Points>

## Who decides what

The kit never decides a price, a payment result or an identity check. It shows what your host decided.

| Concern | Owner | What the kit does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price, fees, limits, quote expiry | Host/backend | Renders the snapshot; asks for re-approval when terms change |
| PIN/OTP/biometric challenge | Host auth SDK | Shows the challenge state; never receives the secret |
| Payment status, idempotency, refund | Host/backend/provider | Renders authoritative snapshots; never infers success |
| Polling, recovery, lifecycle | Host coordinator | Exposes callbacks; widgets never poll on their own |
| KYC decision, document validation | Host/backend | Shows progress, structured errors and the result |

The full table, including input and consent, is on [Host-owned decisions](/concepts/host-owned/).

## Questions

<Points variant="faq">
  <Point title="Does Rakit UI process payments?">
    No. It is UI only. Your backend and payment provider create, authorize and settle payments, and enforce idempotency. The kit displays the snapshots they return.
  </Point>
  <Point title="Which payment provider or backend does it need?">
    Any. You map your provider’s responses to the kit’s models, such as `PaymentAttempt` snapshots, in your own adapter. [Host-owned decisions](/concepts/host-owned/)
  </Point>
  <Point title="Which platforms are supported?">
    iOS (iPhone, iPad) and Android (phones, tablets). Flutter web is used only to preview the docs; desktop is out of scope. [Compatibility](/reference/compatibility/)
  </Point>
  <Point title="What does it cost?">
    Nothing. The package is MIT licensed and free to use in commercial apps. See the [licence](https://github.com/naufaldi/flutter_ui_payment/blob/main/LICENSE).
  </Point>
  <Point title="Is it ready for production?">
    It is an early 0.1.0 release with no known production users yet. Under Dart’s versioning convention, a 0.x minor release can include breaking changes. Review the code, test it with your backend, and [report what you find](https://github.com/naufaldi/flutter_ui_payment/issues/new).
  </Point>
</Points>
