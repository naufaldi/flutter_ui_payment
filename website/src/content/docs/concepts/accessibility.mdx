## Status is never color alone

Every status carries an icon, a label and a sentence; color only supports them. The unknown outcome adds a fourth signal, a hatched `StatusBand`, so it cannot be confused with the solid band of an ordinary pending payment. Strike-through is reserved for failed and canceled amounts.

## 200% text

Text scales fully up to 200%, and layouts reflow instead of clipping:

- Label/value rows share a line when they fit (value at the end edge); otherwise the value wraps flush left.
- Amounts never break across lines. Headline amounts scale down rather than hide a digit, and headline totals are capped at 1.6× scale.
- Above 1.5× text the summary headline is omitted, because the Total row and the pinned bar already show the total.
- On the status screen the one action that resolves an unknown payment stays on the first screen at 200%. Virtual account instructions move after the actions above 1.5× text.
- Long file names keep their start and extension (`ktp_demo_ret…ake.png`) instead of breaking mid-word.

## Targets, labels and announcements

- Touch targets are at least 48 logical pixels.
- Controls have semantic labels. Amounts are spoken as plain digits ("102500 IDR") through `MoneyFormat.spoken`.
- Status changes are announced through live regions; video loading, buffering and finished states are announced too, and the seek slider speaks "0:04 of 0:08".
- Two "Edit" buttons on the KYC review are spoken as "Edit: Your details" and so on, so screen-reader users can tell them apart.
- `PrivacyMask` hides sensitive content and excludes it from semantics while obscured.

## Focus

- A neutral 2 px focus ring appears on keyboard focus only, on the core and secondary controls. It is never brand-colored. This is verified by widget tests; static screenshots cannot show it.
- Drawers and sheets close with the scrim, Back or Escape and return focus to what opened them. Opening the drawer moves focus into it; hidden navigation is not in the focus order.
- Destructive confirmations put initial focus on the safe choice (Cancel).
- Disabled navigation items and action rows stay visible with their reason, but are not focusable.

## Reduced motion

Under `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` every kit duration collapses to zero, the busy state stays a static icon, and video autoplay is blocked. See [Motion](/concepts/motion/).

## What is still yours

The PRD's edge matrix (X02, X03) is exercised in the example's scenario picker, but your app owns its own screens around the kit: safe areas, keyboard insets in your layouts, and OS-level screenshot protection on sensitive pages.
